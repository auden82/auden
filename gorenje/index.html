02 / My proposed approach

A simple promise.
A joined-up launch.

“Life Simplified” is a clear starting point: show what a product makes easier, not just what it does.

For the UK small domestic appliance launch, I would build the plan with Product, Sales and the Emerging Channel & Operations Manager, informed by the range, audience, budget and operational readiness.

01

Make the benefit clear.

Understand the UK consumer, category and competitors. Define the positioning, proposition and messaging within the global brand direction, then agree the launch calendar, budget and success measures.

Pre-launch
02

Make it worth discovering.

Build a content calendar around useful product demonstrations, lifestyle stories and trading moments. Test creator-led content on TikTok and Meta, adapting assets for TikTok Shop, affiliates, Amazon and D2C. Select paid social, search, video, display, SEO and CRM/email activity against audience needs and commercial priorities.

Launch
03

Make the next step easy.

Keep website content and product information clear, current and consistent. Review the journey from campaign to product page and purchase with ecommerce and operations colleagues, identifying barriers to engagement and conversion.

Consumer journey
04

Learn, report and improve.

Track brand awareness, traffic, engagement, acquisition, conversion and return on investment. Combine campaign and channel data with consumer feedback, report recommendations to stakeholders and refine content and spend after launch.
